Biography

Naruto Uzumaki (うずまき ナルト, Uzumaki Naruto)
is the titular character and main protagonist of
the Naruto franchise.

He was often shunned by the Konohagakure villagers
as he is the host of Kurama, the Nine-Tailed Fox
that attacked Konoha.

Naruto eventually achieves his dream
of becoming Seventh Hokage, marries Hinata Hyuga
and has two children: Boruto Uzumaki and Himawari Uzumaki.
